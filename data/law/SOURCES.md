## Notes

- **Amendments.** The FDL 33/2021 pages are the portal's consolidated text, last updated 29 Jul 2024. The
  portal lists 3 amendments, from 2022, 2023 and 2024. The 2023 one is Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2023 (the PRD source).
  Its change is already in the text: Art. 54(2) lets MOHRE decide claims up to AED 50,000. Amended articles
  (8, 54, 60) carry the marker "النصوص السابقة" (previous texts) on the Arabic page, and the parser strips it. No
  separate FDL 20/2023 file is needed.
- **Effective dates.** FDL 33/2021 and CR 1/2022 both took effect on 2 Feb 2022.
- **How they were fetched.** The portal is behind Cloudflare, which blocks plain HTTP clients (403). The pages were
  fetched with the Firecrawl scraper (Markdown output). The PDFs at `/<lang>/legislations/<id>/download` were not used:
  Arabic PDF text extraction breaks glyph order (flag 17), while the HTML text is clean.
- **Known translation difference.** In CR 1/2022 Art. 30(2), the English reads "temporary employment model *or* if its
  duration is less than a year". The Arabic reads "temporary employment model *if* its duration is less than a year".
  The Arabic prevails.
- **To refresh.** Re-save the four pages in this format, update this table, run `python -m haqqi.rag.parse`, then
  `make db`. `tests/rag/test_parse.py` fails if the committed JSON no longer matches the raw files.
