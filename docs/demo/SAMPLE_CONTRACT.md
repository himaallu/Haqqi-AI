# Sample employment contract (for the demo)

A **synthetic** contract to print and hold up, or photograph, during the pitch. No real company or person. The
demo's contract X-ray is scripted: whatever is photographed or pasted, it shows the 8 findings below, so this
contract matches them line by line. A real contract reader is planned (docs/CHAT_PLAN.md, sprint C4).

---

**EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT**

**Employer:** Bright Mart Supermarkets LLC (example), Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates (mainland)
**Employee:** ______________________ Nationality: ____________ Passport no.: ____________

1. **Position.** The Employee is employed as a **Cashier**, starting **1 June 2024**, on a full-time, unlimited contract.
2. **Salary.** Basic salary **AED 2,000** per month. Housing and transport allowance AED 1,000 per month.
   Total monthly salary **AED 3,000**, paid through the bank.
3. **Working hours.** **9 hours a day, 6 days a week.** Friday is the weekly rest day.
4. **Probation.** The first **6 months** are a probation period.
5. **Annual leave.** **30 calendar days** of paid leave for each year of service.
6. **Notice.** Either party may end this contract by giving **120 days'** written notice.
7. **Visa and recruitment costs.** The cost of the employment visa (AED 5,000) will be **deducted from the
   Employee's salary** in 10 monthly instalments.
8. **Documents.** The Company will **keep the Employee's passport** for safekeeping during employment.
9. **Language.** This contract is written in **English only**.

Signed in Abu Dhabi on 25 May 2024.

Employer: ____________________ Employee: ____________________

---

## What the X-ray shows, and the law behind each line

| # | Term | X-ray | Law |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cashier, basic 2,000, total 3,000 | ✓ | |
| 2 | 9 hours a day, 6 days (54 a week) | ⚠ above the normal 8 a day / 48 a week; extra hours are overtime | FDL 33/2021 Art. 17(1) |
| 3 | Probation 6 months | ✓ the legal maximum | Art. 9(1) |
| 4 | Notice 120 days | ⚠ the law allows 30 to 90 days | Art. 43(1) |
| 5 | Annual leave 30 days | ✓ | Art. 29(1) |
| 6 | Visa cost taken from salary | ⚠ the employer cannot charge recruitment costs | Art. 6(4) |
| 7 | Company keeps the passport | ⚠ not allowed | Art. 13(2) |
| 8 | English only | ⚠ must be Arabic plus a language the worker understands | Art. 66(2) |

After the X-ray, "Use these details to check my case" pre-fills Abu Dhabi, AED 3,000 / 2,000 and the start date
1 June 2024. Then tell a story such as *"On 20 September my manager ended my job the same day, with no notice. My
contract says 30 days' notice."* In live mode the real pipeline calculates the claim (AED 6,229.59 for these facts);
with prepared examples the example case runs.
